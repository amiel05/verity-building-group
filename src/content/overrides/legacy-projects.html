Before we begin

Good partnerships
start with clarity.

Is Legacy Projects a separate nonprofit organization?

Legacy Projects is presented as Verity Building Group’s platform for select community-focused construction work. Any formal structure, funding, or tax-deductibility should be confirmed for the specific partnership before commitments are made.

What types of organizations may be a fit?

Mission-driven housing, veteran-support, and community-serving organizations may be considered when the need, scope, funding, schedule, and responsibilities are clear.

Can suppliers or trade partners participate?

Potentially. Participation must be coordinated through a defined project plan so donated materials, labor, warranties, safety, and scheduling are responsibly managed.

How can an organization start a conversation?

Use the contact page to describe the organization, people served, project location, current phase, desired timing, and the construction need.

Will every request be accepted?

No. Capacity is limited and each opportunity is evaluated for fit, readiness, mission alignment, and the ability to create durable value.
