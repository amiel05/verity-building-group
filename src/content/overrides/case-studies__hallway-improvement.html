Hallway Improvement

Coordinated Refresh for a Historic Building

Nearly 12,000 square feet. Three floors. One coordinated refresh for a historic building.

This three-floor hallway improvement was more than a flooring replacement. Verity removed the old carpet, patched walls before repainting, and matched the new wall color to the existing stairwell finish. The scope also included painting trim and doors throughout the project area, bringing the corridors and shared spaces together with a consistent look.

For comfort underfoot, the new carpet was installed with a thicker pad. Verity also secured a 10-year warranty on the carpet—an important long-term consideration for a renovation of this scale.

Project scope

  • Hallway improvements across all three floors of the historic residential building
  • Nearly 12,000 square feet within the project scope
  • Removal and replacement of the old carpet
  • Thicker carpet pad selected for resident comfort
  • Wall patching and paint matched to the existing stairwell color
  • Trim and doors painted as part of the finish refresh
  • 10-year warranty secured on the carpet
