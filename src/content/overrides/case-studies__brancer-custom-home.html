Brancer Custom Home

Crafted for Luxury Living

Three bedrooms. Three and a half bathrooms. Just over 3,000 square feet. Delivered on time and on budget.

The Brancer custom home brings warm materials and purposeful spaces together. White oak flooring runs through the home, while white oak kitchen cabinets and a Taj Mahal countertop anchor the kitchen. A butler’s pantry adds room for the work of everyday life, and a custom gas fireplace gives the living area a focal point.

A steel front entry door sets the tone on arrival. At the rear, a 16-foot sliding door opens to a large covered patio, extending the main living space outdoors. The home also includes a three-car garage.

The primary shower features two showerheads and a large seating area. Above the primary bedroom and bathroom, more than 300 square feet of unfinished storage offers the option to convert that space into an additional bedroom with an en-suite bathroom.

Project highlights

  • 3 bedrooms and 3.5 bathrooms across just over 3,000 square feet
  • White oak flooring and kitchen cabinetry with a Taj Mahal countertop
  • Butler’s pantry and custom gas fireplace
  • Primary shower with two showerheads and a large seating area
  • More than 300 square feet of unfinished storage with potential for a future bedroom and en-suite bathroom
  • Steel front entry door, 16-foot sliding door, large covered rear patio, and three-car garage
  • Completed on time and on budget
