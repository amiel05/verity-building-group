Zwilling Custom Home

Custom home construction

Modern Lakefront Living, Shaped Around the View

4,400 square feet of modern design, tailored amenities, and indoor-outdoor living overlooking Lake Norman.

The Zwilling custom home pairs a clean, modern profile with enduring exterior materials. Stucco and stone define the architecture, while stained tongue-and-groove wood soffits and boxing wrap the home in warmth and detail. High-end Pella casement windows, oversized sliding doors, and expansive glass were selected to bring natural light inside and keep the lake views at the center of everyday living.

Inside, the plan balances open gathering spaces with rooms designed for the way the family lives. A chef’s kitchen features a 48-inch range, an oversized island and sink, and a dedicated small-appliance scullery that keeps the main kitchen composed. Gallery-walk hallways feature custom window designs that bring in natural light and frame the lake views as living art, while a home office, home gym, family-friendly guest quarters, and a custom built-in drop zone give each part of the home a clear purpose.

The primary suite includes a custom closet with a folding island. A luxury lighting package, high-end plumbing throughout, a smart-home control panel, and a modern linear fireplace carry the home’s refined material language from room to room.

Outdoor living was planned as an extension of the interior. Floating steps lead toward the lake, while the entertaining area includes a tiled patio and a screened outdoor room finished with custom-stained wood ceilings. The site also includes a wired hot-tub area and a custom dock designed to complement the architecture of the house.

Beyond the primary living areas, covered RV parking includes a wash station and sewer hookup. Professionally designed, low-maintenance landscaping completes the property and preserves more time for life on the lake.
