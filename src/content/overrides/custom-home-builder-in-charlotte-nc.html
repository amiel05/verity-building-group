Local Knowledge Matters

What to understand before building in Charlotte

01

Two coordinated review paths

Projects within the City of Charlotte may require city zoning, land-development, or urban-forestry review in addition to Mecklenburg County building permits and trade permits. Establishing the jurisdiction and required reviews early helps protect the schedule.

02

Homesite feasibility

Setbacks, easements, utilities, drainage, tree-save requirements, access, and existing structures can change what is practical on a property. A feasibility review helps align the home, site plan, and budget before design advances too far.

03

Renovation discovery

For additions and major renovations, existing conditions deserve the same attention as the new design. Structure, mechanical systems, electrical service, moisture, and finish transitions should be evaluated before construction sequencing is finalized.

Official planning resources: City of Charlotte Development Center and Mecklenburg County permitting.

Charlotte Builder FAQs

Questions clients ask before starting

When should I involve a builder in a Charlotte custom home?

Ideally, involve the builder during site evaluation or early design. Early collaboration allows the team to test the design against site constraints, approval requirements, construction methods, schedule, and budget before major decisions are fixed.

Do Charlotte projects need both city and county approvals?

Often, yes. Depending on the address and scope, the City of Charlotte may review zoning or land-development items while Mecklenburg County administers building and trade permits. The exact path should be confirmed for the individual property.

Can you help evaluate a lot before purchase?

Yes. A preliminary feasibility review can identify visible constraints and questions for surveyors, civil engineers, architects, utilities, and reviewing authorities. It is not a substitute for professional due diligence, but it can help you make a more informed decision.

Do you handle major renovations as well as new homes?

Yes. Major residential renovations benefit from detailed discovery, careful sequencing, and early coordination of existing conditions with the proposed design.

How early can you establish a budget?

A conceptual range can begin once the scope, property, and quality level are understood. The budget becomes more reliable as drawings, engineering, selections, and site information are developed.
